Hello, thank you for the detailed feedback. We have fixed both issues in a new build.

1. Sign in with Google / Apple did not respond
On the sign-up screen, the Google and Apple buttons first check that the two required consent boxes (health data and Terms of Use) are ticked. When they were not ticked, the warning was shown at the bottom of the screen, out of view on the device, so it looked as if the buttons did nothing. In the new build the warning appears right below the Google and Apple buttons, telling the user to tick the boxes. Once the boxes are ticked, both buttons open the Google and Apple sign-in. On the "Entrar" (sign in) screen the buttons work directly, without boxes.

2. The link in the confirmation email did not work
The link used to open the app directly, so it failed when the email was opened on a different device (for example a computer). It now opens a web page (yococino.app/confirmar.html) that works on any device, confirms that the account is activated and offers a button to open the app. After that, sign in with the email and password in the app ("Entrar").

3. Demo accounts

- Account with an ACTIVE subscription (Premium + Peques), to review all features: adrianpedrazag+prueba@gmail.com.
