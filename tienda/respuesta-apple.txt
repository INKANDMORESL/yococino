2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Yococino is a meal-planning app for adults (18+) in Spain. From the user's goal, activity, kitchen appliances, allergies and dislikes it builds a personalised 30-day menu of home-made Mediterranean recipes and the shopping list. It saves time and money and helps people eat balanced meals while avoiding their allergens. It is not a medical service: with pregnancy, diabetes, kidney or thyroid disease or eating disorders no menu is generated and a professional is recommended. Weight goals require BMI >= 18.5 and a moderate deficit with a fixed minimum.

3. ACCESS
Demo account (Premium + Peques active): adrianpedrazag+prueba@gmail.com, password in Sign-in Information. Tap "Entrar", enter it under "o con tu email" and tap "Entrar".
- Inicio: today's meals; "Ver menú completo" opens the calendar.

- Compra: shopping list. Recetas: library and "Mis recetas" (save from TikTok). Progreso: weight and nutrition.
- Perfil: "Mi suscripción", legal links, account deletion.
New users get a 7-day free trial managed by the app. Sign-up with email, Apple or Google.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase (EU servers): login, database, server functions. Sign in with Apple and Google. Apple In-App Purchase via RevenueCat. Anthropic Claude (AI): writes and moderates recipes; no personal data is sent. Google Gemini (AI): dish photos. Expo: builds.

5. REGIONS
Available only in Spain, in Spanish. Same features for all users.

6. REGULATION AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a medical service; menus are general nutritional guidance (Terms of Use) with the safety limits above. Health data is stored only with explicit consent, never used for ads or analytics, and can be deleted at any time. Recipes saved from TikTok are rewritten by the app from the public text only; the video is never shown. No protected third-party material.
User content: every saved recipe is moderated automatically (offensive, non-food, dangerous or advertising content is rejected; recipes with nutrition warnings stay private). Any recipe can be reported with "Denunciar esta receta": it is hidden at once for that user and our team reviews it within 24 hours. Terms of Use section 5: zero tolerance for objectionable content. Contact: "Contacto" in the app or info@yococino.app.
