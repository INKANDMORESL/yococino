2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Yococino is a Spanish meal-planning app for adults (18+) in Spain. Based on the user's goal, activity level, cooking appliances, food allergies and dislikes, it prepares a personalised 30-day menu of home-made Mediterranean recipes, keeps it always 30 days ahead, and builds the shopping list automatically. It solves the daily "what do I cook today?" problem, helps people eat in a balanced way, avoid their allergens and save time and money on shopping. It is not a medical service: for pregnancy, diabetes, kidney or thyroid disease or eating disorders, the app does not generate a menu and recommends seeing a professional. Weight goals are limited to a healthy BMI (≥ 18.5) and a moderate calorie deficit with a fixed minimum.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not a medical or health service and does not provide diagnoses or treatment; recipes and menus are general nutritional guidance, as stated in the Terms of Use. It applies safety limits (adults only, healthy BMI, moderate deficit, allergens always excluded, no menu for medical conditions). Health data (allergies, conditions, weight) is only stored with explicit consent, is never used for advertising or analytics, and can be deleted at any time. Recipes saved from social networks are rewritten by the app (only the public text is read, never the video), and the original video is not shown. The app does not include protected third-party material.

User-generated content: users can save recipes from TikTok links. Every saved recipe is automatically moderated before being stored (objectionable, non-food, dangerous or advertising content is rejected); recipes with nutrition warnings stay private to the user. Any recipe can be reported with "Denunciar esta receta" at the end of the recipe screen: it is hidden immediately for that user and our team is notified and reviews it within 24 hours. The Terms of Use (section 5) state zero tolerance for objectionable content. Users can also contact us from "Contacto" in the app or at info@yococino.app.
